# Prompt 04: Client Onboarding Email

# Prompt Text
Write a short welcome email to Chau, who just signed up as a new client through our website, KERB Creative Agency. The email should sound like it's from a real person, not a company. In this email will include the submit link for the client to send to. If they don't have one yet, then there will be a short questionnaire for them to fill out. The questionnaire should include these:
- Project type (e.g., video, photography, branding, campaign)
- Timeline/deadline
- Budget range
- Reference materials or inspiration (links, mood boards, past work you like)
- Key goals for this project
After they send the brief/fill out the questionnaire, mention that our team will schedule a kickoff call within 2 business days. Keep it casual but professional.

# Intended Workflow/Task
Send a quick welcome email right after a client signs up. 

# Problem Being Solved
This reduces the wait time, no need to manually write an email.

# Automation Potential
could run automatically whenever a new client signs up through the website or via email, generating an onboarding email within seconds and sending it directly to the client.

# Risks and Limitations
Must double check before sending to the client. No further risks to be found yet.
