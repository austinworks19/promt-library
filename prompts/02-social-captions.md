# Prompt 02: Social media caption
# Prompt text: 
You are a social media manager for a creative production company. Based on this video description: "Ananas Football brings their first-ever match jersey collection. What we want people to focus on isn't color, but something more core: the construction (pattern), the fabric, and the tactile feel. A colorless concept — only ivory and soft black." Write a caption for Instagram (under 150 characters) and suggest 8 relevant hashtags. Match the tone to premium, fashion-forward (football as theme, not focus), incorporating Vietnamese football culture into brand identity. Do not invent statistics, claims, or product features that weren't in the description.

# Intended workflow/task: 
Auto-generate caption + hashtag drafts once a video is finished editing.

# Problem being solved:
Captions are written manually each time, causing delays and inconsistent tone.

# Automation potential:
Could be triggered automatically when a video is marked final.

# Risks and limitations:
May use unrelevant hastags and not refelct the current trends. The brand tone and voice could be altered or shifted if not specified prior to posting. Always need to double check.
