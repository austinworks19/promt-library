# Prompt 01: Client Brief Summary & Production Checklist

# Prompt Text
"You are a production coordinator at a creative agency. Read the following client brief: [PASTE CLIENT BRIEF/EMAIL]. Summarise it into: (1) Project goal in one sentence, (2) Key deliverables with formats (e.g., 30s video, 3 social cutdowns), (3) Deadline and any milestone dates mentioned, (4) Budget constraints if stated, (5) A checklist of pre-production tasks needed (crew, location, equipment, permissions) based on the brief".

# Intended Workflow/Task
turning a client brief into a structured and cohesive document so the production team can understand the brief easier.

# Problem Being Solved
Client usually sends a very long brief, with inconsistent formats and unclear requirements. 

# Automation Potential
could run automatically whenever a new brief lands in a shared inbox or client portal, generating a first-draft production checklist within seconds and feeding into project management tools.

# Risks and Limitations
misinterpretation of the client brief due to its being ambiguous or unclear requirements. producers or account excecutive should always double check the budget and legals aspects, especially in a project involves big budget or complex. This should not be a substitution.
