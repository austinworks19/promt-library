Summarise into: (1) What went well, (2) What didn't go well or caused delays, (3) One or two concrete process changes to consider for next time. Keep it factual and specific — avoid vague statements like 'communication could be better' unless the notes explain what actually happened. Do not assign blame to individuals; focus on the process.

### What went well
