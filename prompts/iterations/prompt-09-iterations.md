# Version 1:
Write a pitch outline for this client, without giving a fixed price based on this client's brief: Local coffee brand wants a rebrand launch video, budget around $10-15k, wants something modern and fun, no fixed deadline yet but hoping to launch within 2 months. Never worked with an agency before.

# Output:
**Pitch Outline**
1. Introduction
   * Brief introduction to your agency/team.
   * Acknowledge the client's goal of launching a modern, fun rebrand.
2. Understanding the Brief
   * Rebrand launch video for a local coffee brand.
   * Create excitement around the new brand identity.
   * Target launch within the next two months.
3. Creative Direction
   * Proposed concept and visual style.
   * Tone, storytelling approach, and how the video reflects the new brand.
   * Reference mood or inspiration.
4. Production Approach
   * Pre-production (creative development, planning).
   * Production (filming, talent/location if needed).
   * Post-production (editing, color grading, sound, motion graphics).
5. Deliverables
   * Hero launch film.
   * Social media cutdowns (if appropriate).
   * Optional photography or additional assets.
6. Timeline
   * Discovery and creative development.
   * Pre-production.
   * Shoot day(s).
   * Editing and revision rounds.
   * Final delivery before the target launch window.
7. Budget Approach
   * Explain that the scope will be tailored to their goals and available budget.
   * Present optional production levels or add-ons rather than a fixed quote.
   * Mention that a detailed proposal will follow once the creative direction and deliverables are confirmed.
8. Next Steps
   * Discovery meeting to discuss the brand, audience, and vision.
   * Finalize scope and timeline.
   * Prepare a formal proposal and production schedule.


# Version 2:
"A potential client has sent us this brief/RFP: [Local coffee brand wants a rebrand launch video, budget around $10-15k, wants something modern and fun, no fixed deadline yet but hoping to launch within 2 months. Never worked with an agency before.]. Draft a pitch outline structured as: (1) Understanding of the brief, (2) Proposed creative approach, (3) Suggested deliverables and rough scope, (4) Questions we should ask before finalising a quote. Do not commit to specific prices, timelines, or promises we haven't already discussed — flag anything that needs internal sign-off first with ⚠️."

# Output:
1. Understanding of the Brief
* Produce a launch video to introduce the coffee brand's new identity.
* Creative direction should feel modern, fun, and engaging.
* The client has indicated a target budget of **$10–15k**.
* The intended launch window is within the next two months, though no fixed deadline has been set.
* As this is the client's first time working with an agency, the process should be clear and collaborative.
2. Proposed Creative Approach
* Develop a concept that highlights the refreshed brand personality through vibrant visuals and authentic storytelling.
* Focus on creating a hero film that works across digital and social platforms while capturing the atmosphere of the brand.
* Use a contemporary visual style, energetic pacing, and lifestyle-driven moments to position the rebrand as fresh and memorable.
* ⚠️ Final creative direction should be confirmed after a discovery session and internal creative review.
3. Suggested Deliverables and Rough Scope
* One hero rebrand launch video.
* Social cutdowns adapted from the hero film.
* Basic pre-production, production, and post-production, including concept development, filming, editing, color grading, and sound design.
* Up to an agreed number of revision rounds.
* ⚠️ Final deliverables, production scale, crew size, locations, talent, and post-production scope should be confirmed before preparing a formal proposal.
4. Questions to Ask Before Finalising a Quote
* What is the primary objective of the rebrand launch?
* Who is the target audience?
* Where will the video be used (social media, website, paid ads, in-store, etc.)?
* Has the new brand identity already been finalized?
* Will talent, locations, or products need to be sourced?
* Are there any existing brand guidelines or visual references?
* Are there any mandatory launch dates or campaign milestones?
* What deliverables beyond the hero video are expected?
* Who are the key stakeholders, and what is the approval process?
* ⚠️ Confirm the final scope and production requirements internally before providing pricing or committing to a production schedule.

# What changed
The second prompt is more detailed and specific than the first. Instead of simply asking for a pitch outline, it defines a clear structure, instructs the model not to commit to prices or timelines, and requires any assumptions or pending decisions to be flagged with ⚠️. This results in a more professional and client-ready response while reducing the risk of making unsupported commitments.
