# Prompt 09: New Business Pitch Outline Generator

# Prompt Text
"A potential client has sent us this brief/RFP: [PASTE CLIENT BRIEF OR CALL NOTES]. Draft a pitch outline structured as: (1) Understanding of the brief — show we listened, in our own words, (2) Proposed creative approach — a rough direction, not final ideas, (3) Suggested deliverables and rough scope, (4) Questions we should ask before finalising a quote. Do not commit to specific prices, timelines, or promises we haven't already discussed — flag anything that needs internal sign-off first with ⚠️."

# Intended Workflow/Task
Turning an inbound brief or RFP into a first-draft pitch outline the team can react to and shape, instead of starting from a blank page under time pressure.

# Problem Being Solved
New business pitches are often rushed and win/lose on response speed, but building the first draft from scratch (especially the "understanding of brief" section) eats into the limited time available to actually develop the creative idea.

# Automation Potential
could trigger as soon as a qualified lead/brief is logged in the CRM or inbox, giving the business development lead a structured starting point within minutes instead of hours.

# Risks and Limitations
Should never generate actual pricing or firm timelines — this is a legal/commercial risk if sent to a client without proper review; creative direction suggested is only a rough starting point and shouldn't be mistaken for the agency's actual creative pitch; over-reliance could make pitches feel templated if the "understanding of brief" section isn't personalised further by a human.
